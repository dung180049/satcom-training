Why the First Stage Dominates?
According to Friis's cascade formula. Any thermal noise generated inside the first stage (F_1, the LNA) enters at the very front of the chain and gets amplified alongside the desired signal by every downstream stage. Conversely, the noise introduced by later stages (F_2, F_3) is mathematically divided down by the cumulative linear gain preceding them. Because our LNA provides a massive +30 dB gain (G_1 = 1000 linear), the Downconverter's noise contribution is shrunk by a factor of 1,000, and the IF Amplifier's noise is divided by G_1*G_2 = 1000 * 0.251 = 251. That is why adding 1 dB of noise to the LNA increases total system noise by nearly a full decibel (doubling T_e from 80.1 K to 174.6 K), whereas adding 6 dB of noise to the IF amplifier barely nudges the total noise figure by 0.16 dB
